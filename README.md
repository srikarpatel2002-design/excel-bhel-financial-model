# BHEL Financial Model (Excel)

A three-statement financial model of Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL) for FY22-FY26, with ratio analysis and an FY27-FY29 forecast under Base, Best and Worst scenarios.

*File:* BHEL_Financial_Model.xlsx
*Data source:* Screener.in (BHEL standalone), in ₹ crore

## What the model includes
- Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement (FY22-FY26)
- Balance sheet check that confirms assets equal liabilities and equity
- Ratio analysis: growth, margins, ROE, ROA, current ratio, debt-to-equity, interest coverage and working-capital days, each with a one-line interpretation
- Scenario selector (Base / Best / Worst) driving revenue growth and EBITDA margin
- Linked 3-statement forecast for FY27E-FY29E
- Dashboard with charts and summary insights

## Key findings
- Revenue grew from ₹21,211 cr (FY22) to ₹33,782 cr (FY26), about 12% a year.
- In FY24, net profit fell to ₹282 cr, operating cash flow turned negative and interest cover dropped to 0.56x.
- By FY26, net profit recovered to ₹1,600 cr and EBITDA margin reached 7.6%, but inventory days rose from 131 to 164.

## Skills shown
Excel formulas (INDEX-MATCH, IFERROR), financial statement linking, ratio analysis, scenario modelling, charts
